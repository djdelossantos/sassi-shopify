You have 7 days from the day your order is delivered to request a return.

Items must be unused and in their original condition, with tags and packaging intact. We cannot accept bags that have been used, washed, altered, or damaged after delivery.

Return shipping is covered by you — unless the item arrived damaged or defective, or we sent the wrong thing. In that case we cover it, and we will make it right.

To start a return:

  1. Email hello@sassirabbit.com with your order number and what is wrong.
  2. Include a photo if the item arrived damaged or defective — it speeds everything up.
  3. We will confirm your return and send you the return address.

Please do not ship anything back before we have confirmed it. Unconfirmed returns cannot be processed.

Once your return reaches us and passes inspection, we will process your refund to the original payment method within 5–7 business days. Original shipping charges are not refundable.

Exchanges: want a different colour or model? Start a return and place a new order — it is the fastest way to get the bag you actually want.

Damaged, defective, or wrong item: check your order when it arrives. If something is damaged, defective, or not what you ordered, contact us within 7 days with your order number and a photo. We will sort it out — repair, replacement, or refund — and we will cover the return shipping.
